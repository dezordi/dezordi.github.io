Filipe Dezordi

Bioinformatician · MGnify, EMBL-EBI

I’m a bioinformatician with around 10 years of experience. I earned my bachelor’s degree in Biotechnology working on insect mobilomics, then focused on paleovirology in mosquito genomes during my master’s, and on genomic surveillance of SARS-CoV-2 in Brazil during my PhD, under the supervision of Dr. Gabriel Wallau.

Over the past few years, I have had the opportunity to collaborate with several research groups at the Oswaldo Cruz Foundation (Fiocruz), one of the world’s leading public health institutions, where I completed both my master’s and my PhD.

I have also worked at Hospital Israelita Albert Einstein, consistently ranked as the best hospital in Latin America and among the best in the world, and at Genesis Genomics, part of Grupo Fleury, one of the largest medical and diagnostic healthcare organizations in Latin America.

In addition, I briefly collaborated on projects with the WHO Hub for Pandemic and Epidemic Intelligence and with Instituto Todos pela Saúde, a non-profit organization founded in February 2021 to strengthen epidemiological surveillance and help Brazil prepare for outbreaks, epidemics, and pandemics.

I currently work as a Bioinformatician at EMBL-EBI in the Microbiome Informatics team, where I support the MGnify service and contribute to the AEGIS project.
